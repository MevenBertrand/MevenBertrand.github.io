Post-doctoral researcher in computer science at [Inria](https://www.inria.fr/en) and [IRIF](https://www.irif.fr/en/index).

Before that, postdoc at the University of Cambridge, and even before that PhD student in Inria team [Gallinette](http://gallinette.inria.fr/) at the University of Nantes.
<!-- Even before that, studied computer science and mathematics at the [ENS de Lyon](http://www.ens-lyon.fr/DI/). -->

My main interests are dependent type theory and proofs assistants, in particular [Rocq](https://rocq-prover.org/).
I am also quite fond of everything revolving around bidirectional typing.
One of the things I work towards is bridging the gap between the complex implementations of real-life proof assistants
and their theoretic specifications.
