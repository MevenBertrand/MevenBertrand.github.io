I took part to the 
[Chantiers Arts, Technologies et Sciences](https://www.athenor.com/residences-projets-de-territoire-creations/2019-2020/musique-et-mathematiques),
with researcher Bertrand Michel, theatre writers Sylvain Renard and Rémi Chechetto, and technical high school classes and teachers.
The project should originally have happened in 2020, but due to the Covid pandemic it has been extended, and eventually lasted
for three full years.

Sylvain and I have been writing on this experience since, hopefully it will be eventually published…
